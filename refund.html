Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

This policy covers purchases of plans and credits for GenAI Video Studio, AIBuildOS, iXFlexTopology, DemoBot AI and DemoBot AcadTutorial, sold by [Legal business name].

Failed jobs: automatic

If a job fails, or is interrupted by a restart on our side, the credits it used are returned to your balance automatically. You do not need to ask.

Unused purchases: within 7 days

  • You can request a refund of an unused top-up credit pack or plan within 7 days of purchase.
  • For a plan, the refund covers its credits that have not been used. In iXFlexTopology, the unused part of a credit purchase is refunded. In DemoBot AI and DemoBot AcadTutorial, a plan's unused demos or course generations and a pack's unused credits are refunded pro rata.
  • Free bonus credits you claimed are not refundable.
  • Credits that have already been used are not refundable.
  • Duplicate or wrong charges are always refunded in full.

How to request a refund

Email kavirhene@quantaprism.com from the email address of your account, with the payment ID shown in your payment history. We confirm within 2 working days.

When you get the money

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method through Razorpay and usually reach you in 5–7 working days, depending on your bank.

Cancellation

Plans do not renew automatically, so there is nothing to cancel: a plan simply ends at the end of its period. Top-up credits never expire.

Demo mode. While payments run in Razorpay test mode, no real money is charged, so there is nothing to refund.
