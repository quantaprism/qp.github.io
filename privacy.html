This policy explains what personal data [Legal business name] ("QuantaPrism", "we") collects when you use GenAI Video Studio, AIBuildOS and iXFlexTopology and this website, why, and the choices you have. It is written for India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the IT Rules, 2011.

How we use it

  • To provide the Services: run your jobs, store your outputs and keep your account and credits.

We do not sell your data, show advertising, or use tracking or analytics cookies. The Services set one essential sign-in cookie.

AI processing

The AI models behind the Services (speech, translation, transcription, avatars) run on servers we operate. Your content is not sent to third-party AI services and is not used to train models.

Who we share it with

  • Razorpay, our payment gateway, to process payments.
  • Brevo, our email provider, to deliver service emails.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

Where and how long we keep it

  • Data is stored on servers we operate in India.
  • Uploads and outputs are deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Account data is kept while your account is open. Payment records are kept for as long as tax law requires, typically 8 years.
