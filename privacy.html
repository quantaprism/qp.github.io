Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data [Legal business name] ("QuantaPrism", "we") collects when you use CinefyAI, OSifyAI, NetifyAI, ShowifyAI, ScormifyAI, CodifyAI, BookifyAI and KernelifyAI and this website, why, and the choices you have. It is written for India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the IT Rules, 2011.

What we collect

AccountYour email address, a password hash (never the password itself), plan and credit balance.
Sign-in and securityIP address, browser type and time of sign-ins, used for rate limiting, fraud prevention and sign-in alerts.
Your contentFiles you upload and outputs the Services create, such as videos, audio, transcripts, OS images and test reports.
ShowifyAIWebsite addresses or site files you give it and the pages it visits to make your demo; for live bots, the messages your visitors send and the contact details they choose to leave.
ScormifyAIThe topics and source files you give it, the courses it creates and your edits; for students, their name, e-mail, lesson progress, quiz answers and scores, and certificates.
CodifyAIThe task descriptions, code, project files and repository links you give it, and the code, fixes, diagrams and reports it creates.
BookifyAIThe book topics, PDFs and SCORM courses you give it, and the book text, covers and files it creates.
KernelifyAIThe kernel logs, crash reports and memory dumps (vmcore, with the kernel's vmlinux) you paste or upload, the reports it creates, and in a lab what you type into the lab VM, its debuggers and its packet traces.
PaymentsOrder and payment IDs, amounts, status and dates. Card, UPI and bank details are collected by Razorpay, not by us.
SupportMessages you send us by email.

How we use it

  • To provide the Services: run your jobs, store your outputs and keep your account and credits.
  • To take payments and keep records required by tax and accounting law.
  • To send service emails: account verification, password resets, receipts and plan reminders.
  • To keep the Services secure and prevent abuse.

We do not sell your data, show advertising, or use tracking or analytics cookies. The Services set one essential sign-in cookie.

AI processing

The AI models behind the Services (speech, translation, transcription, avatars, demo scripts and chat bots) run on servers we operate. Your content is not sent to third-party AI services and is not used to train models.

Who we share it with

  • Razorpay, our payment gateway, to process payments.
  • Brevo, our email provider, to deliver service emails.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

Where and how long we keep it

  • Data is stored on servers we operate in India.
  • Uploads and outputs are deleted automatically 1 hour after the job that uses them finishes. A short activity log without file contents is kept 30 days so you can see what you did, and sign-in records (time, IP address, browser) are kept 1 year for security.
  • In ShowifyAI, a chat bot whose live bot is switched on is kept, with its visitor conversations, until you switch it off or delete it. In NetifyAI, a lab nobody uses for 3 hours is removed (you get a warning e-mail after 2 hours).
  • In ScormifyAI, courses, enrolments, progress and certificates are kept until the academy deletes them or closes its account; uploaded source files are deleted 1 hour after the course is ready.
  • In CodifyAI, your code, projects, results and history are deleted 1 hour after the job finishes (a project, 1 hour after it was last used); your code runs only in isolated sandboxes with no internet access. If you connect Jira or save a Git access token, it is stored encrypted until you disconnect it or close your account, and code is sent to your Jira or Git host only when you choose to create, comment or push.
  • In BookifyAI, your books (text, covers and Kindle kits) are kept until you delete them, and at most 90 days after you last changed them, because a book is written and edited over days; the PDFs and SCORM courses you upload are deleted as soon as the import finishes, and at the latest 1 hour after upload.
  • In KernelifyAI, the log you paste or upload is deleted when its analysis ends, at the latest after 1 hour; a result you do not save is deleted 1 hour after it was made, and a saved report 30 days after you saved it. A memory dump (vmcore) and vmlinux you upload are deleted as soon as the analysis has read them, at the latest after 1 hour; the crashed machine's host name is left out of the report. A lab VM, its console, debugger output, packet traces and memory dumps are deleted when the lab session ends. Labs run in isolated virtual machines with no network access; KernelifyAI never connects to your own machines.
  • The AI in the Services runs on our own servers: your content is never sent to outside AI services or used to train AI models.
  • Account data is kept while your account is open. Payment records are kept for as long as tax law requires, typically 8 years.
