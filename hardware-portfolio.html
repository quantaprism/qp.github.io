  • Board design and system architecture

03
Process

From requirements to manufacturing handoff.

  1. 01

    Requirements

    Define the product, interfaces, power budget, size, and target cost.

  2. 02

    Architecture

    Select the SoC or processor, key components, and overall board architecture.

  3. 03

    Schematic

    Capture the full circuit design and review it against requirements.

  4. 04

    PCB layout

    Place and route the board, using AI-assisted tools where they help.

  5. 05

    Review

    Design-rule checks, design review, and fixes before release.

  6. 06

    Handoff

    Deliver production-ready files to your chosen manufacturer.
