Resilient networking software, AI products, and hardware design.

QuantaPrism helps enterprises, cloud operators, and networking teams design, validate, and optimize the software stack behind modern networks — from silicon-facing physical interfaces to application-aware routing and policy enforcement. We turn that expertise into products you can use in your browser: GenAI Video Studio, AIBuildOS, iXFlexTopology, and DemoBot AI. On the hardware side, we design the boards behind connected products — board design, IoT devices, System on Chip (SoC) platforms, and PCB design, including AI-assisted PCB design.
